How to use this tool

1. Position tab: Stand at each camera tripod location, tap "Start Averaging", wait 2-5 min. Tap "Stop & Save" and enter a label ("iPhone 15", "Pixel 8", "GoPro 1").

2. Orientation tab: On iOS, tap "Request Permission" first. Aim the phone at your target, tap "Snapshot & Save". This logs raw sensor values — heading will be imprecise, use landmarks instead.

3. Landmarks tab: Walk to each landmark (runway corner, building, tower), tap "Pin Here". Or enter GPS manually from Google Maps for landmarks you can't walk to.

4. Cameras tab: One entry per camera in camera_index order: label = video file stem, device, measured FOV, tripod position, on-axis landmark, stabilisation OFF / exposure locked ticks.

5. Sync tab: Film the UTC clock with each camera at the start; press SYNC EVENT NOW exactly when the board drops (start and end). Optionally stamp each camera's record start.

6. Export tab: Share recipe.json to the laptop, drop it next to raw/ in data/runs/<date_site>/, fill in cameras[].sync_event_seconds from the raw videos, run tools/run_pipeline.py.

All data persists in browser localStorage, works offline after first load.
